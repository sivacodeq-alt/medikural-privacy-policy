Account and data deletion

MediKural is a medicine reminder and tracking app published by Appstobb (developer: Siva Selvam). If you signed in with Google and would like your MediKural account and associated personal data deleted, you can send a request directly to our support contact.

Request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Use the button below to email us. You do not need to sign in to the app to send this request. We may ask you to verify ownership of the MediKural account before acting on the request.

Email account deletion request ↗

Or send an email to appssiva2@gmail.com with the subject MediKural - Account and Data Deletion Request.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the email button above, or write to appssiva2@gmail.com.
  2. Include the Google email address used to sign in to MediKural and say you want your MediKural account and associated data deleted. If you only want specific records deleted while keeping your account, describe those records instead.
  3. We will review your request, may verify that you own the account and will communicate about the deletion process. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment card numbers or detailed medical documents to prove ownership.
  4. After verification, we will handle the associated app data within our control and communicate if any limited records must be retained for legitimate reasons.

What data is covered by an account deletion request?

A verified full deletion request covers your MediKural account and associated data held by the App, including, where present:

  • Firebase Authentication identity connected to your MediKural sign-in and account profile details (for example name, email and account identifier).
  • Cloud medicine records, dosage instructions, schedules, dose-taking history, medicine stock and related reminder preferences.
  • Medicine photographs stored in MediKural's Firebase Storage for your account.
  • Associated support records and other account-linked data that can be identified and deleted, subject to legal or security retention obligations.

Important: The in-app Delete Account button may not remove every previously synchronized cloud record. If you want comprehensive deletion, please use this contact process so cloud records can be reviewed and removed as part of the request. Do not assume that merely uninstalling MediKural, signing out, or deleting your Google Account automatically deletes all MediKural cloud data.

What may remain, and for how long?

  • Information on your device: Guest data and local files on a device we cannot access may remain until you delete them within MediKural, clear the app's device storage, or uninstall it. The deletion request does not remotely erase files stored only on your device.
  • Required records: Limited records may be retained when genuinely necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or resolving the deletion request. These are kept only for the period necessary for that purpose or as required by applicable law. Any specific retention period applicable to your request can be explained in our response.
  • Service provider backups: Google/Firebase systems may retain temporary operational copies and logs subject to their own applicable backup and retention processes. Removing your MediKural account does not delete your underlying Google Account.

There is no general additional fixed retention period that we promise for all deleted MediKural records. Completion timing may depend on identity verification, technical work and applicable obligations; we will communicate the expected next steps when your request is reviewed.

Keep your account but delete specific data?

You can remove individual medicine entries through the App. For deletion of specific cloud records while retaining your account, email appssiva2@gmail.com with the subject MediKural - Specific Data Deletion Request, stating which data you would like reviewed. We may verify your identity before acting.
