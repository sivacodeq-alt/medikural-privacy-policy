Privacy Policy

Clear information about how your medicine reminders, personal records and account data are handled.

Effective date: 16 October 2026Developer: Siva Selvam · Appstobb

MediKural – Medicine Reminder ("MediKural", "we", "our" or "the App") helps people organize medications, schedules, dose history, medicine stock and reminders. This policy explains what information may be processed, why, where it is stored and the choices available to you.

Health information deserves extra care. Medicine names, dosages, schedules, photos and dose history can be sensitive. You can use core features locally. Cloud backup is optional and should only be enabled if you want your records synchronized through Firebase.

1. Information you provide

  • Account and profile: if you register or sign in, your email address, account identifier, sign-in method, display name and, where provided, age. Account authentication may use email/password or Google Sign-In.
  • Medicine and health-related records: medication names, dosage, timings, frequency, special instructions or notes, stock quantity, expiry dates, dose-taking status and history, and related reminder preferences.
  • Photos: medicine or packaging photos you choose to take or select. Signed-in users' photos may be privately backed up to Firebase Storage; guest photos remain stored on the device.
  • Support messages: categories, subjects, messages and information you choose to provide, together with basic app/device diagnostic context needed to handle the request. Avoid sending passwords, OTPs or unrelated medical documents.
  • Other optional information: if additional tracking or subscription features are available in the version you use, relevant details you enter or purchase entitlement information needed to deliver those features.

2. Guest mode, device storage and cloud backup

Medicine records are initially saved on your device, including in local app storage. Guest medication records and guest medicine photos are not automatically uploaded to an account's private cloud backup. Some support submissions can be transmitted to our support system even when you use guest mode.

When an eligible, verified user chooses to enable Cloud Backup, medication records, dose history and selected settings may be synchronized with Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore. Medicine photographs associated with a signed-in user may be stored in private Firebase Storage. Turning off synchronization does not necessarily delete a cloud copy already created. Do not assume that deleting or uninstalling the App removes information already stored in a cloud account.

3. How we use information

  • To display medicines, schedules, dose history, stock status and health-related summaries.
  • To deliver or schedule local medication reminders, notifications and voice alerts you configure.
  • To sign users in, manage profiles and support optional backup and restoration.
  • To process support requests, diagnose errors, prevent abuse and maintain service security.
  • To operate optional advertising or subscription features if they are activated in a future or applicable build.

MediKural is an organizational aid, not a medical provider. We do not use your medication entries to diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or make automated medical decisions.

4. Permissions and device features

With your permission, the App may use notifications and alarms to remind you about medicines, and camera or photo library access when you choose to add a medicine photo. You can change available permissions in your device settings. Some functionality may be reduced if permission is denied. The App does not need microphone access for ordinary medicine-photo selection.

5. Third-party services and advertising

MediKural uses Google Firebase services, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Storage, where the relevant feature is used; Google Sign-In may process your sign-in information. The App also contains Google Mobile Ads / AdMob integration. Advertising is not necessarily active in every build, but if enabled, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions and technical usage information according to their policies and applicable consent requirements. We do not intentionally send your entered medicine names or dose history to advertising providers for ad targeting.

Relevant third-party information: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, and AdMob privacy guidance. Applicable Google Play services may also process data under their own terms.

6. Sharing and access

We do not sell your medicine records or personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate selected app features, including Google Firebase and, where enabled, AdMob. Authorized administrators may access account summaries and support tickets to provide support and maintain service operations. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or needed to protect users and our systems.

7. Security

We use account authentication, encrypted connections provided by service platforms and access rules intended to restrict private cloud records to the relevant account. Photos and medication records stored on your own device also depend on the security of that device. No internet transmission or storage system is entirely risk-free.

8. Retention, deletion and your choices

You can edit or delete individual medication records within the App. You can manage notifications through App and device settings, and switch off optional cloud synchronization. Local guest information ordinarily remains on the device until removed by app functionality, device-data clearing or uninstalling.

When signed in, an in-app Delete Account option may remove your authentication account and certain local data. At present, this action should not be relied on as confirmation that all previously stored Firebase cloud backups, photos, support tickets or other server records have been erased. For a request to delete your account-related cloud data, or to access/correct personal information, email appssiva2@gmail.com with the subject MediKural Privacy or Data Deletion. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. Information may be retained where required by law, fraud prevention or other legitimate obligations.

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to the practical operation of local storage, optional backups and any applicable legal requirements. Third-party platforms may maintain separate logs or backups under their policies.

9. Children's privacy

MediKural is not directed at children under 13. Users under the age of majority should use the App with appropriate parent or guardian involvement where required. If you believe a child's personal information was provided without proper authorization, contact us to request review and deletion.

10. International processing

Depending on the services you use, personal information may be processed on servers in countries other than your own, including through Google services. Applicable data protection requirements and service-provider safeguards govern this processing.

11. Medical disclaimer

MediKural is a medication tracking and reminder tool, not a medical device or substitute for medical advice. Notifications may be delayed, silenced or missed due to device settings, battery restrictions or connectivity. Do not change, start or stop medication based only on App reminders or insights. Always follow instructions from your doctor or pharmacist.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, data practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date on this page and, when appropriate, through the App or other suitable notice.
